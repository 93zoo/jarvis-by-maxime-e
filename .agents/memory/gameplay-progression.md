---
name: Gameplay progression rules
description: Durable rules for talent points, item recycling, and region combat rewards.
---

Player progression awards one talent point for every player-level increase. Legacy saves need a baseline migration so already-earned levels are not left without their new points, while spent talent costs remain accounted for.

**Why:** Talent points were previously tied to skill levels, but the intended progression is easier to understand and balance when it follows the player level directly.

Item melting is destructive and must be confirmed in the UI. Recovery is based on the recipe requirements and the item's quality, with better quality returning a higher fraction of materials.

**Why:** Recycling should be useful without making crafting materials infinite, and the quality of the forged item should matter after crafting.

Region combat is a three-round cumulative dice duel. A win grants materials from that region, Combat skill XP, player XP, and a small exploration increase; a loss still gives limited Combat/player XP but no materials.

**Why:** Combat should be a playable alternative to harvesting, while keeping regional materials and progression tied to the location.
